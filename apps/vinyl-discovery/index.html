Vinyl Discovery

Coming Soon!

Crate digging for the whole Discogs catalog

Vinyl Discovery is a record-digging app built on the Discogs catalog. Pick a genre, a style, a year or a country, and the app turns the results into a playlist that plays each release’s videos from YouTube, one record after another.

Start with a curated pocket, such as 1984 Japanese city pop or 1992 Detroit techno, or build your own search and let it play. When something catches your ear, save it to a YouTube playlist or open the release on Discogs to add it to your wantlist.

Vinyl Discovery is free to use with ten releases a day. A subscription removes the limit and supports the app’s development.

Features

  • Search Discogs by genre, style, year, country and label
  • Turn any search into a playlist that plays release after release
  • Start from curated discovery pockets, from 1970 jazz fusion to 2013 cloud rap
  • Save the track that's playing to your YouTube playlists
  • Open any release on Discogs to add it to your wantlist or collection
  • Keep the player running while you browse with the mini player
